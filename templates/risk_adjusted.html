HOW LIFEOS CALCULATED THIS

Risk-adjusted decision scoring

LifeOS starts with the weighted decision score and reduces it according to the assessed risk. A higher risk therefore lowers the final risk-adjusted score.

Risk-Adjusted Score = Weighted Score × (1 − Risk Score / 100)
