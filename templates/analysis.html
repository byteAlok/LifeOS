HOW LIFEOS CALCULATED THIS

Weighted decision scoring

Each option receives a score from 0 to 10 for every criterion. LifeOS multiplies each score by that criterion's importance weight, divides the contribution by 100, and combines all contributions into a final weighted score.

Weighted Score = Σ (Score × Weight ÷ 100)
